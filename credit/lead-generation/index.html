Lead generation for debt consolidation

The best leads come from people already looking for help with their debt. Here are the channels, ranked roughly by lead quality, and the two things that matter as much as the channel you choose.

Channels, ranked by lead quality

Highest intentLower intent
  1. 1

    SEO content and calculators

    Pages like “consolidate $15K credit card debt” or a payoff and savings calculator attract people actively shopping. It's the cheapest lead source over time, but it takes months to build.

    Low cost per leadSlow to start
  2. 2

    Google search ads

    Target high-intent keywords such as “debt consolidation loan” or “pay off credit cards.” Send clicks to a focused landing page with a soft-pull pre-qualification form, not your homepage.

    High cost per clickStrong conversion
  3. 3

    Lead marketplaces and aggregators

    Firms like LendingTree and Even Financial supply volume quickly, but leads are often sold to several companies at once, so speed to contact decides who wins them.

    Fast volumeShared leads
  4. 4

    Pay-per-call

    Inbound calls convert much better than web forms. You pay only for calls that meet your criteria, such as a minimum call length or debt amount.

    High conversionPay for qualified calls only
  5. 5

    Paid social retargeting

    Facebook, Instagram and TikTok work best for bringing back people who already visited your site or calculator, less so for cold traffic.

    Best for re-engagementWeak on cold audiences
  6. 6

    Partnerships and referrals

    Credit counselors, mortgage brokers, tax preparers and HR benefits programs can send warm referrals. Lenders you partner with may also refer declined applicants.

    Warm leadsTakes relationship building
  7. 7

    Email nurture

    Most visitors aren't ready on day one. Follow up with useful content, like payoff plans and rate updates, until they are.

    Converts earlier trafficNeeds a list first

What matters as much as the channel

Speed to contact

Reaching a lead within minutes rather than hours can multiply conversion rates, especially with shared leads where several companies are calling the same person.

Compliance

  • Get clear, documented consent before calling or texting (TCPA).
  • Avoid “guaranteed approval” claims.
  • In debt relief or settlement, the FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule bans charging fees up front.
  • Consumer lending requires state licensing.
